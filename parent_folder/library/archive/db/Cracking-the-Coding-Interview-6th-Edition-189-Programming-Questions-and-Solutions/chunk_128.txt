Generating a List of Primes: The Sieve of Eratosthenes
The Sieve of Eratosthenes is a highly efficient way to generate a iist of primes. It works by recognizing that
ail non-prime numbers are divisible by a prime number.
We start with a list of all the numbers up through some value max. First, we cross off alt numbers divisible by
2. Then, we look for the next prime (the next non-crossed off number) and cross off alt numbers divisible by
it. By crossing off alt numbers divisible by 2, 3, 5, 7,11, and so on, we wind up with a list of prime numbers
from 2 through max.
