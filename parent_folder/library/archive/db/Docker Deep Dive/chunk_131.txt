Scaling a service
Another powerful feature of services is the ability to easily scale them up and
down.
Let’s assume business is booming and we’re seeing double the amount of
anticipated traffic hitting the web front-end. Fortunately, scaling the web-fe
service is as simple as running the docker service scale command.
$ docker service scale web-fe=10
web-fe scaled to 10
