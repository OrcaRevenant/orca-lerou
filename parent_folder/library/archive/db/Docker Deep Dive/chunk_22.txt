Chapter summary
In this chapter we learned a bit about Docker, Inc. They’re a startup tech
company out of San Francisco with an ambition to change the way we do
software. They were arguably the first-movers and instigators of the container
revolution. But a huge ecosystem of partners and competitors now exists.
The Docker project is open-source and lives in the moby/moby repo on GitHub.
The Open Container Initiative (OCI) has been instrumental in standardizing the
container runtime format and container image format.
