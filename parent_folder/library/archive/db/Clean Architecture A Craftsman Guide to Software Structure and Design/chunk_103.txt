CONCLUSION
As we move forward in this book and cover higher-level architectural principles,
the DIP will show up again and again. It will be the most visible organizing
principle in our architecture diagrams. The curved line in Figure 11.1 will
become the architectural boundaries in later chapters. The way the dependencies
cross that curved line in one direction, and toward more abstract entities, will
become a new rule that we will call the Dependency Rule.
