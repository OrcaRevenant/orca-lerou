CONCLUSION
The dependency management metrics described in this chapter measure the
conformance of a design to a pattern of dependency and abstraction that I think
is a “good” pattern. Experience has shown that certain dependencies are good
and others are bad. This pattern reflects that experience. However, a metric is not
a god; it is merely a measurement against an arbitrary standard. These metrics
are imperfect, at best, but it is my hope that you find them useful.
