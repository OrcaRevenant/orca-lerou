Business rules are the reason a software system exists. They are the core
functionality. They carry the code that makes, or saves, money. They are the
family jewels.
The business rules should remain pristine, unsullied by baser concerns such as
the user interface or database used. Ideally, the code that represents the business
rules should be the heart of the system, with lesser concerns being plugged in to
them. The business rules should be the most independent and reusable code in
the system.
