COMPONENT COHESION
Which classes belong in which components? This is an important decision, and
requires guidance from good software engineering principles. Unfortunately,
over the years, this decision has been made in an ad hoc manner based almost
entirely on context.
In this chapter we will discuss the three principles of component cohesion:
• REP: The Reuse/Release Equivalence Principle
• CCP: The Common Closure Principle
• CRP: The Common Reuse Principle
