The great claim to fame of the Datanet 30 was its capability to drive a large
number of asynchronous terminals at relatively high speed. That’s exactly what
ASC needed.
ASC was based in Lake Bluff, Illinois, 30 miles north of Chicago. The Local 705
office was in downtown Chicago. The union wanted a dozen or so of their data
entry clerks to use CRT4 terminals (Figure A.4) to enter data into the system.
They would print reports on ASR35 teletypes (Figure A.5).
