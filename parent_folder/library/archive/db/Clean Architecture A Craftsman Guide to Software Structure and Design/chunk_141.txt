WHAT IS ARCHITECTURE?
The word “architecture” conjures visions of power and mystery. It makes us
think of weighty decisions and deep technical prowess. Software architecture is
at the pinnacle of technical achievement. When we think of a software architect,
we think of someone who has power, and who commands respect. What young
aspiring software developer has not dreamed of one day becoming a software
architect?
But what is software architecture? What does a software architect do, and when
does he or she do it?
