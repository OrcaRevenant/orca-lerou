POLICY AND LEVEL
Software systems are statements of policy. Indeed, at its core, that’s all a
computer program actually is. A computer program is a detailed description of
the policy by which inputs are transformed into outputs.
In most nontrivial systems, that policy can be broken down into many different
smaller statements of policy. Some of those statements will describe how
particular business rules are to be calculated. Others will describe how certain
reports are to be formatted. Still others will describe how input data are to be
validated.
