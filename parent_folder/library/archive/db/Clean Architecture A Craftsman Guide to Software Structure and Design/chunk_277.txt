THE MISSING CHAPTER
By Simon Brown

software, composed of classes and components with well-defined boundaries,
clear responsibilities, and controlled dependencies. But it turns out that the devil
is in the implementation details, and it’s really easy to fall at the last hurdle if
you don’t give that some thought, too.
