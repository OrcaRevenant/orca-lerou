WHAT IS DESIGN AND
ARCHITECTURE?
There has been a lot of confusion about design and architecture over the years.
What is design? What is architecture? What are the differences between the two?
One of the goals of this book is to cut through all that confusion and to define,
once and for all, what design and architecture are. For starters, I’ll assert that
there is no difference between them. None at all.
