CONCLUSION: THE MISSING ADVICE
The whole point of this chapter is to highlight that your best design intentions
can be destroyed in a flash if you don’t consider the intricacies of the
implementation strategy. Think about how to map your desired design on to code
structures, how to organize that code, and which decoupling modes to apply
during runtime and compile-time. Leave options open where applicable, but be
pragmatic, and take into consideration the size of your team, their skill level, and
the complexity of the solution in conjunction with your time and budgetary
constraints. Also think about using your compiler to help you enforce your
chosen architectural style, and watch out for coupling in other areas, such as data
models. The devil is in the implementation details.
